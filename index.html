Our story

Ninety-six years,
one family

TAT Ristorante Di Famiglia has been cooking Italian food in Columbus since 1929, when Pete and Philomena Corrova opened their first restaurant in the old Flytown neighborhood. The dining room has moved a few times since. The recipes came with it.

We brought the first pizza to Columbus all those years ago, and the recipes have barely changed since. Four generations of the same family still work the dining room and the kitchen.
