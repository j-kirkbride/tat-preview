Accessibility

Everyone eats here

We want this website, and our dining room, to work for every guest. If something here gets in your way, tell us and we will fix it.

What we aim for

We work to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA — the standard most widely used to judge whether a website is accessible. Every page can be used by keyboard alone, every photograph has a text description, text contrast exceeds the AA requirement throughout, and the site works with a screen reader, with the page zoomed, and on a phone.

Where we fall short

Ordering, reservations and gift cards are handled by outside companies on their own websites. We chose them, but we do not control how they are built. If any of those give you trouble, call us on (614) 236-1392 and we will take your order, booking or gift card over the phone. We will also read you the menu and answer questions about ingredients.

In the restaurant

If you have questions about parking, the entrance, restrooms or seating, call us before you come and we will tell you honestly what to expect. Service animals are welcome.

Tell us when something is wrong

Let us know what you were trying to do, and what device or software you were using if you can.
